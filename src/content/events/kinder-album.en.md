On 14 October, UCPN Social brought together Ukrainian artist Kinder Album and young participants from the Chance for Change Foundation for a workshop exploring how emotions, experiences, and personal stories can be expressed through images.

The workshop began with a conversation about artistic language, metaphor, and the different meanings that images can carry. Participants then developed their own paintings, translating personal ideas and feelings into visual compositions.

Rather than teaching a particular artistic style, Kinder Album encouraged experimentation and individual expression. The resulting works reflected different personalities, experiences, and ways of seeing the world.

The workshop was organised by UCPN in collaboration with 3.6.9 Gallery and Store as part of the *Artists Working with Trauma and Healing* programme.
