On 14 October 2026, 3.6.9 Space hosted the first event of the “3.6.9 Social” series, organized with the support of the Ukrainian Culture Professionals Network (UCPN): an artistic practice “How artists code meaning in an image” with Kinder Album together with teenagers from the “Shans na Zminu” foundation.

The participants explored how to use metaphor in artistic language, transform an emotion into an image and tell a story about feelings without words. Drawing on personal experience, open discussion and dialogue, the teenagers created their own works, which the artist described simply as “so cool”.
