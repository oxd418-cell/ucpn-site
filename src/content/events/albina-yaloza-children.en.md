On 14 November, UCPN Social welcomed ten displaced children facing challenging life circumstances to a second linocut workshop with Ukrainian artist Albina Yaloza.

This time, the workshop focused on fears and personal talismans. Albina invited the children to think about what frightens them, what gives them strength, and which symbols or images might offer a sense of protection.

The participants transformed these ideas into linocut compositions and printed their designs onto white tote bags, creating personal objects they could take home and use.

The workshop became a space for creativity, trust, and connection. One of its most memorable moments came at the end, when the children spontaneously embraced the artist.

The event was organised by UCPN in collaboration with 3.6.9 Gallery and Store as part of the *Artists Working with Trauma and Healing* programme.
