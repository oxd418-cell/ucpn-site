“Essays: Culture & Memory” is a series of texts and conversations that brings together Ukrainian and international authors, artists and thinkers to reflect on culture as an infrastructure of memory, care and public life during and after war.

The project looks at how artistic and cultural practices shape the way we remember, how we care for each other and how we imagine the future. Each text is written as a dialogue or polemic between two authors, creating a wider conversation about the role of culture in a time of ongoing violence and transformation.
