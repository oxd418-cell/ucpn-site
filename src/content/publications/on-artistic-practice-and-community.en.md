UCPN’s first project is a series of essays developed through conversations between Ukrainian and international cultural practitioners.

The project will bring together three pairs of authors, each exploring a distinct topic related to culture during and after the war. Rather than contributing separate texts to a collective publication, the authors will work as interlocutors, developing their ideas through dialogue, shared reflection, and an exchange of perspectives.

The choice of this format reflects one of UCPN’s central ideas: meaningful cultural collaboration begins with connections between people, their experiences, and their different ways of understanding the world.

For UCPN, the value of this project lies not only in the essays themselves, but also in the professional relationships and conversations that emerge through their creation.

Our role is to facilitate these encounters, provide space for collaboration, and bring the resulting conversations to a wider audience.

This first project is deliberately modest in scale, but it represents the approach we hope to develop through UCPN: creating opportunities for people to meet, exchange ideas, and initiate collaborations that can continue beyond individual projects.

**The first essays and their authors will be announced soon.**
