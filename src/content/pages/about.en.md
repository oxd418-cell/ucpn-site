# About Us

**Ukrainian Culture Professionals Network (UCPN)** is an independent initiative supporting grassroots, artist-led, and non-institutional cultural and artistic projects.

We are interested in initiatives that emerge through horizontal collaboration between artists, curators, and cultural organisations — outside established institutional structures or at the intersection of different communities and disciplines.

UCPN brings people and projects together, creates space for collaboration, and supports artistic practices that raise questions, engage with complex subjects, and do not always fit into conventional formats.

## Areas of Focus

- **Horizontal connections** between Ukrainian and international artists, curators, and cultural organisations.
- **Art and the experience of war** — engaging with loss, trauma, and lived experiences.
- **Art in public space** — artistic practices that move beyond conventional exhibition formats.
- **Cultural heritage** — exploring and reinterpreting heritage and cultural narratives.
- **Memory and memorialisation** — artistic approaches to personal and collective memory.
